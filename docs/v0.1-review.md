# PMC 2670 Firefight — v0.1 review

A review of the codebase at v0.1 (main at `c7181fa`, 27 September 2026) in three parts: design, architecture
and quality; conformity to the rulebook; and performance. Each finding was checked against the code, and the
ones marked *reproduced* were run. Nothing was changed while reviewing.

Book references are to the rulebook Markdown (`PMC_2670.md`), by line.

## 1. Summary and suggested order

The engine is cleanly separated from the view: `src/engine` and `src/rules` have no DOM or view references, so
the same engine runs authoritatively on the server, and networking is intent-based with server-side dice. The
game follows the book closely — all 200 unit profiles match it exactly, as do the hit tables, suppression,
activation, scenario schedules, solitaire tables and the campaign economy — and the engine, AI and campaign are
fast (a whole battle takes 0.35–1.4 s of CPU). The weaknesses are the multiplayer server's trust boundary; rules
that drifted between two copies of the same logic, are measured from the wrong point, or take away a choice the
book gives the player; and the cost of drawing.

At a glance:

| Part | High | Medium | Low | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Design and quality | 5 | 7 | 3 | 2.1 and 2.5 reproduced |
| Rules conformity | 3 | about 20 | many | high includes medium-high (3.14, 3.31); plus one deliberate departure (3.26); Appendices 1–2 not offered |
| Performance | 4 | 3 | 2 | measured |

Suggested order:

1. **Server security** — 2.1 to 2.4, then 2.6 and 2.7: cheap, and the only real exposure.
2. **Rules that change games most** — rout counting (3.1); reinforced walls (3.27); Adrenaline Rush and Last
   Stand (3.31, 3.33); objectives held from the edge (3.20); Battlefield Insertion as a choice (3.19); charges
   in solitaire scenarios (3.14); and the Xenotripod salvage bug (3.32).
3. **Campaign options that do nothing or too much** — 3.2–3.4, 3.34, 3.35.
4. **Performance** — idle redraw (4.1), drawing at screen resolution (4.2), a faster table bake (4.3).
5. **The rest of the rules findings**, then **architecture** (2.8, 2.9) when those files are next touched.

## 2. Design, architecture and quality

### High

**2.1 One malformed URL crashes the server** — *reproduced*
`server/static.js:46` and `server.js:68` call `decodeURIComponent` on the raw path inside the request handler,
unguarded. `curl http://127.0.0.1:18787/%E0%A4%A` throws `URIError` and the process exits, ending every lobby
and live battle.
*Fix:* wrap both decodes and answer 400; add a top-level try/catch in the `createServer` handler.

**2.2 Stored script injection through a company name**
`src/view/panels.js:1006` writes log lines into the page unescaped (`'<p>' + l.text + '</p>'`), as do the
game-over card and `sideName()` (`:854-855`). Log lines carry `cfg.nameA`/`nameB` (`engine.js:535`, `:883`),
which come from the player's force name; `protocol.js cleanForce` only strips control characters and clamps to
40 characters, so `<img src=x onerror=…>` fits and runs in the other player's browser when the game starts.
`panels.js` already has `esc` (`:303`).
*Fix:* escape `l.text`, `l.math` and the over-card text; reject `<` and `>` in names.

**2.3 Anyone can become the host, or take a dropped player's seat**
A player is whoever the `playerId` in `hello` says (`server/lobby.js:180`); `isHost` compares ids (`:62`); and
`Room.view()` sends every seat's id and the `hostId` to everyone in the room, watchers included (`:76`, `:81`).
A watcher can reconnect as the host and kick, change settings or start; a `hello` with a dropped player's id
takes their held seat (`:184-190`).
*Fix:* issue a secret reconnect token at `welcome`, keep public ids apart from authentication, and refuse an id
that is already connected.

**2.4 The campaign API has no authentication, allows any origin, and listens on the whole network by default**
`server.js:28` defaults `HOST` to `0.0.0.0` (the header comment and `SERVER.md:8` say it is opt-in). `PUT` and
`DELETE /campaign/:name` (`:74-83`) need no credentials and send `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`, so any page
open on the network can overwrite or delete campaigns.
*Fix:* default to `127.0.0.1`; drop the wildcard CORS or use an allow-list; require a per-campaign token for
writes.

**2.5 Multiplayer campaign battles can never start** — *reproduced*
The dossier saves with `C.forSave` (`dossier.js:124`), which drops `companies.B` on purpose
(`camp-rivals.js:43`); `server/table.js:111-112` reads `camp.companies[side]` and never calls `C.rehydrate`.
A campaign built with `newCampaign` + `foundRivals` + `forSave` makes `buildConfig()` throw "the campaign has
no company for seat B". `servertest.js` has no campaign table.
*Fix:* call `C.rehydrate` in `Campaigns.get` or `buildConfig`, and add a test.

### Medium

**2.6 Every snapshot reveals the secretly mined terrain piece**
The Terrorist doctrine's mined piece is chosen secretly (`engine.js:441-477`), but `save.js:146` puts
`mined.side` and the piece index in the snapshot `table.js` sends to both seats and to watchers.
*Fix:* build a per-seat view in `Table.flush`/`resync` that removes hidden fields.

**2.7 Some intents let one seat change the other side**
`autosplit` (`engine.js:1455`) resets both sides' reserve splits, gated only on the deploy phase. `select`
(`:1409`) has no side or phase check outside the battle, and `ui` is shared by both seats, so one player can
clear the other's mode, moves and hints mid-deployment.
*Fix:* limit `autosplit` to the sender's side; gate `select` on `mayDeploy`/`mayLay`, or keep `ui` per seat.

**2.8 The engine is a hand-maintained forwarding layer**
`create()` in `engine.js` is 1,791 lines, with 167 `(KIT_X || kitX()).fn()` stubs and 13 kit factories each
passed a hand-written bag of dependencies (e.g. `kitAI`, `:676-693`); the same shape repeats in `rules.js`
(1,824 lines), `campaign.js` and `game.js`. Wiring is inconsistent (`kitSave` falls back to `require`,
`kitAI` does not), adding a function means editing three places, and `intent()` alone is 331 lines.
*Fix:* give each kit an explicit context (`state`, `ui`, `view`) and a registry to reach the others; turn
`intent` into a table of `{ guard, handler }` keyed by intent name.

**2.9 The view writes its caches onto engine state**
`draw.js:139-157`, `:181`, `:269`, `:404-421` and `:552` set `scene`, `ground`, `structs`, `props`, `remains`,
`baking`, `objKey`, `structsDirty` and `tset.baked` on `B.state`, which in local play is the live engine state
(`net.js:333`). `save.js:124` keeps a hard-coded skip-list, already missing `objKey`, `structsDirty` and
`tset.baked`. Local and remote clients also see different state shapes, so multiplayer-only bugs slip past the
mostly-local browser tests.
*Fix:* keep render caches in a view-owned `WeakMap`; serialise with an allow-list.

**2.10 Rules coded twice, and already drifting**
The swap allowance is in `engine/swaps.js:17-20` and `rules/camp-contract.js:59-61`. `onTable` differs:
`scenarios.js:203` excludes reserves, `engine/deploy.js:18` and `solitaire.js:228` do not. `COLOURS` is copied
in `engine.js:25` and `protocol.js:52`, whose header says it is the one place so neither side can drift.
*Fix:* one copy of each, in `R` or `P`.

**2.11 Campaign aftermath failures vanish**
`game.js:198` runs `try { window.PMC_ONFINISH(m.report); } catch (e) { }`; if the aftermath throws, the battle
result is dropped without a word. Resume swallows replay errors the same way (`net.js:254`). `Campaigns.put` is
last-writer-wins, so a stale dossier `PUT` can overwrite the server's own result.
*Fix:* pass the callback in, log and show errors, and add a revision check to `PUT`.

**2.12 Save versions are written but never read**
`campaign.js:19` sets `VERSION = 1` and nothing reads `camp.v`. The local battle book (`net.js:159`) replays
intents under a seeded `Math.random`, so any rules change makes the fingerprint differ, and the saved battle is
then discarded silently (`:259-261`).
*Fix:* a migration step keyed on `v`; stamp the build into the book; tell the player when a save cannot resume.

### Low

**2.13 Stale comments** — `protocol.js` lists server messages `snapshot` and `events`; the server sends `turn`,
`refused`, `started` and `over` (`table.js:209`, `:227`). The `server.js` `HOST` comment (2.4).

**2.14 Test hooks ship to players** — `hooks.js` installs about 86 `window.__*` functions in the production
page; `__actionState` (`:146`, `:368`) and `__uiMode` (`:151`, `:285`) are each defined twice, so the first of
each is dead.

**2.15 Test gaps and slow tests** — `server.js` routing is inline and cannot be imported, so it is untested;
nothing tests malformed URLs, identity, hidden information or a campaign table (2.1, 2.3, 2.5, 2.6).
`clienttest.js` takes 241 s of a unit run that otherwise needs about 20 s: move it to the slow set or stub its
canvas baking.

## 3. Rules conformity

Checked against the rulebook. `src/rules/ruletext.js` is the game's own tooltip text ("what the rule does here,
in this implementation… not the rulebook's wording"), so it is not a source of truth for this section.

### Confirmed defects

**3.1 Rout counts army picks, not units** — high
Book l.1277: a force is routed when it loses "½ of the enemy units… their Unit Tiers are of no importance".
`routed()` (`scenarios.js:233-236`) takes the starting count from `army.length`, the picks, but the units left
from `state.units`. A turret set is one pick but 2–4 units (`engine.js:338-348`); the free Complex Teleport
Network turrets (`engine.js:364`) and solitaire `extraUnits` are never in the starting count. A 6-pick army
with a four-turret set (9 units) routed only after losing 6.
*Fix:* count the start from `state.units`, with the same drop-pod and Expendable exclusions.

**3.2 Demolisher never applies** — medium
Book l.2813: "+4 Firepower bonus when shooting at destructible terrain or troops hiding in it". `shootTerrain`
checks `has(a, 'Demolisher')` (`destruct.js:135`), a rule name no profile carries; the upgrade is a campaign
flag (`shoot.js:225` reads it correctly with `campFlag`). An MCV with the upgrade rolled 11 against a wall that
needed 15.
*Fix:* `campFlag(a, 'demolisher')`; also check the "troops hiding in it" half.

**3.3 Superior Self-repair System does nothing** — medium
Book l.2821: "can re-roll failed repair rolls". The `selfRepair` flag is set by `effects()` and never read;
`repair()` (`damage.js` ~256) has no re-roll. It is the only campaign flag never read.
*Fix:* a re-roll branch in `repair()`.

**3.4 Passengers of a downed aircraft get 9 TP and are listed as dead** — medium
Book l.2702: "Troops on-board survive, but get 5 TPs". The engine sets the passengers' `models` to 0
(`damage.js:193`), so `tpFor` also adds "lost more than half its soldiers" (`camp-contract.js:209`) and every
soldier goes on the casualty list. The existing test only checks `tp >= 5`.
*Fix:* skip the losses line and the casualties when `line.aboardDowned` and the aircraft is recovered.

**3.5 The Lifter can shoot and strafe** — low-medium
Book l.3481 gives the Lifter no Firepower ("-"), and l.493: without one, a unit "cannot shoot at all". The data
has `fp: 0`, not `null`, so `canShoot`, `shoot`, the Strafe action (`actions.js:203`) and the AI
(`ai.js:226`) all accept it: 201 of 2000 shots hit.
*Fix:* `fp: null` for the Lifter, or block both for `Unarmed`.

**3.6 The Command Vehicle offers only Coordinate** — low-medium
Book l.1517: the Command Unit on board "may perform one of their special actions (e.g. Coordinate or an action
allowed by any other special rule)". `engine.js:1029-1041` and `arrive.js:153` offer Coordinate or nothing;
Hack, Markerlights and Regain Control are never offered.
*Fix:* offer the Command Unit's other special actions.

**3.7 Brave is applied too early** — low
Book l.2755: "ignores 1 Suppression point from each ranged attack". Brave is taken off in
`resolveShootingHits` (`damage.js:81`), and only if the hit table gave SP; Incendiary doubling and Suppressive
Fire's +2 are added afterwards in `shoot()`. Incendiary in woods gave 8 SP to a plain unit and 6 to a Brave one;
a Suppressive Fire volley that rolled all "Steady" still gave a Brave unit the full +2.
*Fix:* apply Brave (and Courage Under Fire) once, to the attack's final SP.

**3.8 "Surrounded, but Steady" counts enemy reserves** — low
Book l.2781: "one additional die for each enemy unit within 18"". `rules.js:1476-1481` does not skip reserves,
which are parked at (-1,-1): a unit at (6,6) with 3 enemy reserves got +3 dice.
*Fix:* skip `reserve` units and anything with `x < 0`.

### The code disagreeing with itself

**3.9 The assault odds shown differ from the assault rolled** — medium
`assaultOdds` (`shoot.js:255`), shown at `panels.js:480` and `:544`, leaves out modifiers `assaultRound`
(`assault.js:248-266`) applies: Holy Fury +2, Pheromone Markers, the Overgrown Bug's +4 against vehicles,
Metal-covered Talons, Fierce Attacks and the Sappers' +4. A Sandworm against a vehicle shows +8 and rolls +12;
Fanatics with Holy Fury show +6 and roll +8.
*Fix:* one modifier function shared by the preview and the roll, as `shotMods` already is for shooting.

**3.10 Two pieces of code score objectives** — low (latent)
`scoreObjectives` (`endphase.js` ~240-250) does not check `R.holdsGround`; `holderOf` (`scenarios.js:213`)
does, so in Secure and control a "No Objectives" drop pod could claim or deny an objective.
*Fix:* have the End phase call `SC.holderOf`.

### Checked and found to follow the book

- **The 12 Suppression Point cap** — book l.517: "The maximum number of Suppression points a unit may accumulate
  is 12". Morale 4–5 units therefore stay Broken rather than flee, as written.
- **Stealth** — book l.1619: "+1 for distance above 6", +2 for distance above 12"", as the code does.

### Open

- **Field Medics while Suppressed** — book l.521: Suppressed and Broken units cannot use passive skills that give
  "bonuses to another units", naming Field Medics. The code lets Suppressed medics treat their own wounded,
  which that wording arguably allows.
- **Style Bonus** — see 3.34: the book applies it to every kill, assault included.

### Book-first pass

Five reviews read the book section by section, to find what a check starting from the code cannot: rules never
implemented, and rules implemented wrongly where the tooltip was written to match.

#### Army lists (PMCs, Rebels, Space Bugs, Xenotripods)

The unit data is clean. A script parsed all 200 profiles in the book (PMC 83, Rebels 52, Bugs 26, Xeno 39) and
matched every one to the game's 200: none missing, none extra, and no stat differs (Tier, Size, Movement,
Firepower, Range, Defence, Assault, Morale or Structure, turret-set size). Every per-army and per-Priority-Level
cap is enforced, and all 20 preset armies are legal. Of 49 profiles whose rule lists read differently, about 40
are the same rule written another way (e.g. "Destructive" as "Destructive Weapon").

**3.11 A dug-in gun can still be towed away** — medium
Book l.2952 (p. 95): "The gun in defensive position cannot be turned or embarked by transport vehicle."
`canEmbark` (`rules.js` ~1162) and `boardableFor` (`save.js` ~85) never check `u.dugIn`: a dug-in `rmedart`
beside an `rltv` can embark.
*Fix:* in `canEmbark`, refuse Stationary Artillery that is dug in.

**3.12 Aircraft can tow Stationary Artillery in play** — medium
Book l.2946-2948 (p. 95): the guns are "transported (towed) by transport vehicles". In play `canEmbark` lets any
transport take a gun, an Armed light shuttle included; at deployment `boardableFor` already requires a ground
vehicle, so the two disagree.
*Fix:* require `veh.cls === 'vehicle'` for Stationary Artillery in `canEmbark`.

**3.13 Rules added to profiles that the book does not give them** — low
- *Fire beetle* (l.3720, p. 118) carries "Overgrown Bug": it counts toward the 3-per-PL Overgrown cap, gets +4
  in assault against vehicles and is worth 25 biomass. Plausible for a Structure 7 bug, but the book is silent;
  keep it with a comment, or remove it.
- *Medical drone unit* (l.2120, p. 72) carries "Drone unit", so it counts toward the drone limit and clears all
  Suppression on a rally; already noted as deliberate in `data.js`.
- *Light engineering vehicle* and *EW vehicle* (l.2296, l.2340) carry "Ground vehicle", and the bug giants carry
  vehicle or flying classes, consistent with the Overgrown rule (p. 116). Harmless.
- Some group placements and one name differ from the book (Rapid insertion platform, EW and Medic teams,
  Mujahideen's alternative names); no cap depends on them.

**Ambiguous readings**
- *Xeno turret cap* (l.4122): "no more than 3 turrets (or turret sets in case of Teleport turrets)" read
  literally counts each Defensive turret; the code counts a set as one pick.
- *PMC Command Unit cap*: `cmd4` has no "Command Unit" rule but counts toward the 1-per-PL cap.
- *Last Stand* (p. 95): "+4 to their Defence… in terrain which grants a Defence bonus" — the code raises the
  cover bonus to +4 rather than adding +4 to it.
- *Core Gamma troopers* carry the 1-per-PL note and the higher Gammas do not; the code follows the book.

#### Solitaire, co-op and the appendices (book l.4752–5484, pp. 146–167)

Solitaire matches the book closely: the phase order, activation from the furthest unit, the behaviour bands
and modifiers, Cumbersome, each scenario's modifier, the commando and OpFor tables (a 200-roll probe per
faction, Battle Tier and Priority Level broke no limit), counter reveals, landing zones, the VIP rolls and +2
Damage, Decapitation's leaders, the civilians and Evacuation's auto-rally, and Ambush!'s 75% check. The defects
are in how rules interact.

**3.14 Charges ignore the scenario movement limits** — medium-high
Book l.5044 (p. 154): OpFor units "cannot enter the safe zone"; l.4938 (p. 151): units "cannot voluntarily
move more than 12" away". The limits are enforced through `canStand` (`ai.js:350`) and `moveOK`
(`solitaire.js:561`, `:835`), but the charge code (`canAssault`/`chargeReach`, `assault.js:25-66`, and the
move into contact at `:161`/`:164`) never asks: a probe found a legal charge onto a player unit 8.5" inside
Evacuation's safe zone. In Protecting the VIP a charge can take a unit past 12", or the VIP past 6".
*Fix:* refuse a charge whose contact point fails `scen.moveOK`, for the AI and the player.

**3.15 "No enemy within Range" also demands line of sight** — medium
Book l.4808 (p. 147): "+2 if there are no enemy unit's within the active unit's Range". `ai.js:413` applies it
when `bestTarget` found no shot, which needs line of sight, a fire arc, Specialisation and minimum range; an
enemy in range behind a building still gives +2, pushing the OpFor towards Offensive and Kill Them All!.
*Fix:* a plain distance test against the enemy units on the table.

**3.16 The Ambush! column runs off the table** — medium
Book l.5102 (p. 156): the column is spaced "no closer than 1" and no further than 3" from each other".
`solitaire.js:950-958` starts at x = 4 and adds 3–5" a unit, clamped at the table edge: a 14-unit column put
two units at x = 47 and overlapped three pairs. In co-op the column overflows in 55/300 rolls at Battle Tier II,
278/300 at IV and 300/300 at V.
*Fix:* close the gaps towards 1", then fold the column or centre it.

**3.17 Evacuation caps OpFor arrivals** — low-medium
Book l.5042: each unit arrives on a 5+ "from a random entry point", with no per-point limit (unlike the VIP
scenario). The code allows six a turn (`solitaire.js:812`) and one per entry point.
*Fix:* its own arrival rule, without the used-entry check.

**3.18 Smaller solitaire defects** — low
- A revealed counter can lose its unit for good: the counter is removed before the unit is placed, and if no
  spot is found within 5" the unit stays in the pool with nothing to reveal it (`solitaire.js:310-317`), which
  can block an OpFor rout in Crushing the Resistance.
- The VIP's statline comes from the PMC list for Bug and Xenotripod commandos (`solitaire.js:492`); Decapitation
  (`:609`) already accepts Leader Bugs and Alpha squads.
- Command Unit profiles are barred from commandos (`solitaire.js:86`); the book (l.4882) only drops the Command
  Unit *rule*.
- Sabotage's "within 12" of an objective" is measured centre to centre, ignoring the objective's 2" size
  (`solitaire.js` ~917).
- When an OpFor cannot meet the anti-vehicle requirement (l.4870) it is rolled without it, silently: a Bugs
  OpFor at Battle Tier I failed 300/300, as the Bugs list has no such unit at Tier I–III.

**Not implemented**
- *Offered but incomplete:* co-op battles inside a campaign (l.4890, "currency and EXP… halved").
- *Not offered:* all of Appendix 1, Close Encounters (corridors, doors, hidden movement, Opportunity Fire,
  its three scenarios); Appendix 2, Other Worlds (gravity, atmosphere and anomalies — the planet setting only
  chooses a terrain generator, `gen.js:549-593`); from Appendix 3, "There can be only two!", "Bad, worse, the
  bookkeeper" and multi-figure bases. Appendix 3's propulsions and mounts are implemented correctly.

**Ambiguous readings**
- Sabotage's objectives "3… Add 2 per Priority Level" is read as 3 + 2 × (PL − 1); it could be 3 + 2 × PL.
- Ambush!'s "more or less equal" halves and 6" gap are not enforced; "towards the enemy" is read as towards
  the road.
- Also: Cumbersome applied to the modified total; VIP arrivals stay at 2+ after turn 17; co-op landing zones
  measured centre to centre; civilian control re-rolled in the Beginning phase; Defensive and Neutral pick the
  best shot rather than "the biggest threat"; the OpFor's three machines per Priority Level.

#### Set-up, scenarios and general special rules (book l.1132–1638, pp. 45–59)

Priority Level scaling, the Command Unit and swap limits, the six terrain tables, every scenario's length and
turn limits, Find & Secure's searches and reserves, Invasion's waves, and most general special rules
(Advanced Protection, Anti-tank and Anti-aircraft, Battle Armour, Gauss, Incendiary, Suppressive Fire, Field
Medics, Jammers, Hackers, Limited Fire Arc, Minimum Range, Specialisation, Cumbersome, Supporting Fire,
Markerlights) follow the book. The gaps are geometry, and choices the book gives the player that the code takes.

**3.19 Battlefield Insertion is forced, and a deployed inserter stays "in reserve"** — medium
Book l.1507 (p. 56): units with the rule "**can** arrive… using a special procedure". `deploy.js:59-72` puts
every such unit in reserve, up to half the army, outside the player's reserve split, so it cannot be set up on
the table. A `deploy` intent for one returns ok and places it, but leaves `reserve` true (`engine.js:1744-1762`):
a unit on the board that the rules treat as off it.
*Fix:* let the player release inserters to normal deployment; have `deployAt` clear `reserve` or refuse.

**3.20 Objectives are captured from their centre** — medium
Book l.1281 (p. 49): a unit "within 4" from the marked objective… area objectives, count units within them
first". `holderOf(x, y, 4)` measures from the centre (`scenarios.js:208-219`, called at `:407`, `:520`), though
a Find & Secure site is a 4" piece and a landing zone an 8" circle. A unit 3.9" from the site's edge may search
it (searches measure from the edge) but cannot hold it; a unit 2" outside a landing zone holds nothing.
*Fix:* measure to the edge, and count units inside an area objective first.

**3.21 Shooting at terrain leaves out the shooting modifiers** — medium
Book l.1531 (p. 57): destroyed if the "final D10 roll result (with all modifiers applied) is 15 or more".
`shootTerrain` (`destruct.js:128-136`) adds only Firepower, models and Demolisher, not Fire!'s +1, half range's
+2 or height; the same test against a unit sheltering in terrain uses the full total (`shoot.js:307`).
*Fix:* build it from the non-target parts of `shotMods`.

**3.22 Sappers' bonus applies when defending, and cannot be declined** — medium-low
Book l.1601: the bonus is for Sappers who **assault**; l.1607: the player may order a standard Assault instead.
`sappingAt` (`assault.js:237-240`) checks only the rule and the first round, so a defending Sappers unit gets
+4 and can blow in its own wall.
*Fix:* attacker only, with an opt-out.

**3.23 Scenario set-up differences** — low to low-medium
- *Invasion defender's share on the table* rounds down (`scenarios.js:453`, `floor(n/3)`); the book allows "up to
  1/3" (l.1407) and rounds divisions up (l.465): with 4, 5, 7 or 8 units, 1, 1, 2, 2 instead of 2, 2, 3, 3.
- *Hostile takeover's first wave* may enter from all four edges (`scenarios.js:714`); the book (l.1473) has it
  enter from one edge the attacker chooses, and only the second part from any edge (l.1481).
- *Hostile takeover's fortifications* are 6–10 sections dropped at random (`scenarios.js:673-696`); the defender
  "may place up to 10" (l.1467).
- *Demolish reserves* can come on 6" deep (`scenarios.js:597-598`); reserves are placed "up to 4" from the table
  border" (l.551).
- *The SAM system* measures 12" from the objective's centre (`scenarios.js:635`), about 10" from its edge
  (l.1447).
- *Demolish and Hostile takeover* delete terrain near the objective (`scenarios.js:550`, `:670`); the book has no
  such step.
- *The D3 scenario roll* is offered for every battle; the book (l.1152) allows it only at Tier I–II and/or
  Priority Level 1.
- *Mountain "Mine"* always gets a hill (`gen.js:92`); the book says it "may be on a hill" (l.1245).
- *Command Units cannot be swapped* (`swaps.js:23`, `:33`); the book (l.1160) only requires the same Tier.

**3.24 The AI can strand a unit whose insertion failed** — low
`ai.js:73` sets `reserve = false` with the unit at x = -1 while the log says it "stays in reserve", so it never
arrives; the player's path (`arrivals.js:405`) correctly keeps it in reserve.

**3.25 Tooltip disagrees with the book: Expendable** — low
Book l.1541: the collars explode and the unit is removed. The tooltip says it "fled rather than died, so in a
campaign it comes back", and the code does that (`endphase.js:274`, `damage.js:343`).

**Not implemented**
- Players nominating the Secure & Control objectives (l.1345): they are placed automatically.
- Alternating terrain changes between the players (l.1156) and the smaller modular table (l.1174).
- A Destructive Weapon against a unit behind a **high** wall (l.1533): `shelterOf` skips pieces that block
  sight; and "can always be destroyed if near" for Indirect Fire with a Destructive Weapon (l.1565).

**Ambiguous readings**
- Inspiring Presence on itself: "other units with this rule" suggests it benefits; the code excludes it
  (`rules.js:1409`).
- Who may Demolish the objective: p. 54 says all units, p. 49 attacking infantry; the code uses the latter.
- Also: landing zones' 8" and open-ground checks made at the centre only; insertion's 12" gap ignoring unfound
  search sites; the SAM firing on the defender's own aircraft; no landing Suppression for infantry landing in a
  transport; industrial "medium walls" drawn as high walls.

#### The battle (book l.429–1131, pp. 27–44)

The core is faithful to the book, numbers and edge cases included: the shooting, assault, vehicle and aircraft
hit tables, overkill and knock-out; a vehicle's explosion (Firepower 3 + Tier within 4"); repairs; the
Suppressed, Broken and flight thresholds; Overwhelming Numbers; Crossfire and its exclusions; defensive fire,
the assault order, Broken defenders and the six-round fall-back; vehicle turning and reversing; and embarking
limits. The departures are geometric.

**3.26 A unit at a terrain edge — a deliberate departure, kept**
Book l.990–992 (p. 42): a unit "partially in the open and partially in a wood counts as being in the open when
it is shot at, but as being in a wood when it moves" — the worse case each way. The game uses one, more lenient
rule for both: a unit is in terrain when at least half its rim is (`space.js:408` `kindsUnder`), which is also
how its models are drawn. So a token centred 0.3" inside a wood gets the +2 cover, and one 0.2" outside the
edge pays no movement penalty.
*Decision:* kept. On a screen a unit cannot be placed with the precision of a model on a table, so the book's
worst-case reading would punish players for placement they cannot control; the half-rim rule gives one answer
that matches what the table shows.

**3.27 Tier III–V vehicles drive through reinforced walls and destroy them** — medium
Book l.1029–1031, l.766 and the table at l.1085: reinforced walls "cannot be crossed" and are not destructible.
`terrainBars` (`move.js:71`) and `crushOnMove` (`destruct.js:226`) test only the terrain kind and ignore
`reinforced` (`isDestructible` does check it): a Light destroyer reached 152 spots beyond a reinforced wall, and
brought it down.
*Fix:* `isDestructible(r)` in `crushOnMove`, and treat a reinforced piece as a barrier in the movement search.

**3.28 The 36" sight limit is measured centre to centre** — medium-low
Book l.447 and l.529: distances are base to base, and units "can see within 36"". `space.js:457` compares
`centreDist`, so sight ends at about 34" base to base: a mortar (range 48") cannot see a target 35" away, and
the Bio-plasma thrower (range 36") cannot hit one at 35.5".
*Fix:* compare `unitDist`.

**3.29 Suppressed units cannot move into cover behind a low wall** — medium-low
Book l.700–702 (p. 34): a Suppressed unit may move "to any area **or linear** terrain… which provides a Defence
bonus". `safeSpot` (`moves.js:304`) uses `coverAt`, which never counts low walls, and so a unit already behind
one does not count as safe and may move on.
*Fix:* also accept spots where `coverFor` gives low-wall cover against at least one enemy.

**3.30 Smaller battle-rule defects** — low
- *The Reserve phase does not alternate* (l.549, "apply the Alternate activation rule"): all of A's reserves
  arrive, then all of B's (`arrivals.js:192`), whoever has the initiative.
- *A Strafing run can end over terrain too high to hover over* (l.898): its endpoints skip `canStand`
  (`actions.js:430`, and the AI's `bestStrafe`, `ai.js:328`).
- *Passengers bailing out of an Abandoned or burning vehicle* are not marked as disembarked (`damage.js:206`,
  `:213`), so they may board again the same turn (l.797, l.800, l.837) and a Cumbersome Weapon may still fire.
- *Broken-minded rounds down* (`rules.js:1473`); the book rounds every division up (l.465), so Morale 5 rolls 2
  dice instead of 3.
- *The Transport tooltip* gives only half the rule: nor may a unit get off on the turn it got on (l.837), as the
  code already enforces.

**Not implemented**
- Surrendering in the End phase (l.722).
- Leaving the table voluntarily, counting as fled (l.568): movement is clamped to the board.
- The multiplayer battle rules (l.1108–1128).
- Removing casualties and unit coherency (l.525, l.617): each unit is one token.
- Wrecks only block sight; units can still move through them.

**Ambiguous readings**
- Aircraft ignore terrain for sight, both shooting and shot at, and the 36" limit (`shoot.js:85`); the book only
  says they shoot "over other units". A parked aircraft blocks sight between two ground units.
- Height's +2 Firepower is left out of Basic Firepower shots; a vehicle's explosion, being Basic, gets no
  half-range +2.
- A unit firing at an arriving reserve must also have it within Range (`deploy.js:162`).
- A Tier III+ vehicle crossing a high wall pays no movement penalty, as the table calls high walls "impassable"
  rather than giving one.

#### Campaign (book l.2529–2870, 3486–3600, 3959–4102, 4551–4751)

The economy and bookkeeping follow the book: recruit, company and unit promotion costs; the EXP and TP tables
(PR Masters, Mental Training, consecutive battles, rest); the honour cap and draw, Rapid Training Methods; the
negotiation and plunder doctrines; the free units of each faction; and the leaders who earn no EXP or TP. Every
honour, trauma, Adaptation, Flaw, Rite and Infamy flag is read somewhere, bar Superior Self-repair (3.3). A probe
of 40 campaigns × 3 rivals × 30 turns found the rival AI always legal, except that its catch-up money (`catchUp`,
`deepen`) comes from outside the payment rules, by design.

**3.31 Adrenaline Rush gives the side an extra activation, not the unit two actions** — medium-high
Book l.2753 (p. 88): "Once per battle the unit can make two actions in a row." `moves.js:33-36` only adds to
the side's streak; the unit then acts once and is spent, so `afterChain` (`engine.js:1090`) gives the extra go
to another unit.
*Fix:* let the same unit take a second action (clear `activated` once after its first).

**3.32 Xenotripod Improved Engines also acts as Advanced Emergency Systems** — medium
Upgrade #1 is Advanced Emergency Systems for PMCs (l.2809, aircraft salvaged on 2+) but Improved Engines for
Xenotripods (l.4690). `camp-contract.js:247` checks `upgrades.indexOf(1)` whatever the table, so an `xstrike2`
with Improved Engines is salvaged on 2+.
*Fix:* check the `advEmergency` flag through `effects(entry).flags`.

**3.33 Last Stand costs the unit's activation, and a Broken unit cannot use it** — medium
Book l.2757: "Once per battle the unit can remove all its Suppression points", not as an action. `moves.js:21-31`
spends the activation, and it is reached only from the action menu (`engine.js:1179`), which Broken units never
see (`eligible()`, `engine.js:972`) — when it matters most.
*Fix:* make it free, usable in the Rally phase or at activation, Broken or not.

**3.34 Style Bonus does nothing in assaults** — medium-low
Book l.2777: "Whenever the unit kills an enemy soldier ('Man down!' or similar), the enemy unit gets 1
additional SP." Only shooting adds it (`damage.js:76`); `resolveAssaultHits` (`:89-112`) does not.
*Fix:* the same `out.sp += out.casualties` in assault.

**3.35 Smaller campaign defects** — low to low-medium
- *Nanobots cannot be fitted to aircraft* (`campaign.js:342`, `ground: true`); unlike upgrades #1 and #2 the
  book (l.2815) does not limit it to ground vehicles, and aircraft do make repair rolls (l.922).
- *The disband check ignores faction and doctrines* (`camp-company.js:294`): a swarm is judged on the PMC
  composition table, so disbanding a `bsmall` at Swarm Tier II is refused though the swarm could still field a
  legal army (l.2653); Non-conventional Army (O2) is ignored too.
- *Fungi Symbiosis counts every non-bug unit as human* (`assault.js:321`): Xenotripods, vehicles, drones and
  turrets also spawn Infected Humans (l.4009, "every human unit destroyed").
- *Efficient Spawn Cycle also discounts promotions* (`campaign.js:524`); the book (l.4005) discounts spawning
  only, where Smuggler names both.
- *Advanced Control System* (Xenotripod aircraft #10, l.4706) works only for the AI (`ai.js:244`); player
  aircraft get no turn after moving.
- *Rite of Concentration is spent automatically* on the first D10 of 5–8 (`shoot.js:296`); the unit "may"
  double it.
- *Mimicry and Underground Advance* give Battlefield Insertion to the first ⌈n/4⌉ infantry (`engine.js:386-393`);
  the book lets the player choose "up to ¼".
- *`takeHonour` and `takeUpgrade` accept any number*: the same honour twice, or an aircraft upgrade on a ground
  vehicle. The UI and rival AI only offer legal choices, so only the API or an edited save can do it
  (l.2753, l.2809).

**Not implemented**
- Advanced Control System for the player's aircraft (3.35).
- The battle AI never uses Adrenaline Rush or Last Stand.

**Ambiguous readings**
- Effective Resource Utilisation (l.4594) literally counts 1–3 as "6"; the code uses 4.
- Recruits may also be promoted to other Basic troops; the book (l.2671) lists four destination groups.
- Iron Discipline and Broken-minded say "regroup"; the code applies them to every rally roll.
- Passengers of an aircraft that is not salvaged are removed, though l.2702 says "troops on-board survive".
- Also: drone ground vehicles lost on a Catastrophic explosion (l.2704 has no such exception); turrets count as
  Tier I for kill EXP; Enhanced Genetic Memory is also offered for units disbanded at their tenth Infamy;
  Infamy of Degeneration is rolled at the next aftermath; Rob the Rich and Drug Dealer round down; Complex
  Teleport Network turrets are clamped to Tier II–IV.

## 4. Performance

Measured in headless Chromium without a GPU, so drawing runs on the main thread; a desktop with a GPU will be
faster, but the proportions hold, and this is what low-end and software-rendered devices see.

### Key numbers

| What | Result |
|---|---|
| Frame, 1280×800, default zoom | 16.7 ms p50 (14.4 ms of work) |
| Frame, 1920×1080, default zoom | about 33 ms p50, about 30 fps (21–31 ms of work) |
| Frame, zoomed in | 4–7 ms of work |
| Idle with an aircraft on the table | 40–70% CPU |
| AI step | p50 2 ms, p95 13–32 ms, max 84 ms |
| Whole battle (engine and AI) | 0.35–1.4 s |
| Battle-start table bake | 1.2–3.3 s |
| Menu freeze | 1.3 s after load, then 1.1–1.6 s every 10 s |
| Start-up, 1× CPU | about 350 ms (dev: 83 requests, 2.7 MB; site bundle 1.3 MB, 399 KB gzipped) |
| Start-up, 4× CPU and slow network | 2.1 s dev, 1.4 s site bundle, 2.3 s single file |

### High

**4.1 The board redraws continuously when idle if any aircraft is on the table**
`draw.js:226-231` tests for *any* animated unit on the table, off-screen included, and redraws the whole board
every 60 ms. Waiting for the player with helicopters in play, the main thread was 60–70% busy; only counting
on-screen units took it to 0.6%.
*Fix:* count only animated units on screen (as fire and haze already do), or repaint just their boxes over a
cached frame.

**4.2 Zoomed out, every frame is built at full table resolution and then shrunk**
`draw.js:435` clamps the supersample to at least 1, so at the default zoom (0.55–0.75) each frame is composited
at up to 5.9 Mpx from the 3176×1856 plates (`:446-448`) and scaled down (`:650`). Allowing it below 1 cut work
at 1080p from 21–27 ms to 15–16 ms (28–40%) and brought frames back to 16.7 ms.
*Fix:* draw at screen resolution; keep a pre-scaled static layer per zoom so the ground keeps its smoothing.

**4.3 Baking a table tests polygon distance for every pixel**
`iso-ground.js:488` `depthIn` → `space.js:162` `pieceDepth` runs `pointSegDist` on every edge for every pixel.
`bakeGround` takes 0.75–3.0 s (jungle 2.2–3.0 s, up to 1.2M calls); a rectangle stand-in brought it to
0.57–0.93 s. This is the 1.2–3.3 s battle-start freeze, and the menu's (4.4).
*Fix:* inside/outside once per scanline from the polygon crossings, and an exact distance only near the edge
(about 0.6"), or a coarse distance grid per piece. Expect 2–3× faster.

**4.4 The menu backdrop freezes the page every 10 seconds**
`menu.js:150` `bake()` via `:250` `next()` (`EVERY = 10000`) gave long tasks of 1333 ms at 390 ms after load
(the menu is unresponsive for its first 1.7 s), then 1644 and 1088 ms.
*Fix:* 4.3; bake a screen-sized region at half scale, or bake a few tables once and cycle them.

### Medium

**4.5 Repainting structures stalls and allocates about 24 MB** — `draw.js:138-160` redraws every prop twice
(solid and cut-away) and creates a new 3176×1856 `structsOpen` canvas each time: at start, on every collapse or
barricade, and when objectives change. `drawProp` took 170–240 ms at battle start.
*Fix:* reuse the canvases and redraw only the changed prop's rectangle.

**4.6 Vehicles and aircraft are drawn from scratch every frame** — `iso.js:455` `hullWithLoad` →
`iso-machines.js:458` `drawMachine` was 8% of a 12 s profile at 1080p. Troopers are already sprite-cached
(bounded, about 8.8 MB).
*Fix:* cache hulls by art, side, facing (about 16 steps), status and animation phase.

**4.7 About 94 MB of canvas per battle** — `ground`, `structs`, `structsOpen` and the zoomed-out `pix` buffer
are 23.6 MB each; heavy for phones. The JS heap stays flat at 11–15 MB over a long battle. 4.2 removes the `pix`
buffer at full size, and merging ground and structures removes another (measured −17% frame time at the
current resolution).

### Low

**4.8 The battle log is rebuilt twice per render** — `panels.js:1004` rewrites all 400 entries into `#log` and
`#log-dock`, then reads `scrollHeight`: about 5 MB of `innerHTML` per 20 s and 1.5% of the main thread.
*Fix:* append new entries, trim old ones.

**4.9 Campaign saves store each other-front battle twice** — `dossier-after.js:103,114` keep both
`after.fronts` and `after.elsewhere` (its flattening), about 4.7 KB a turn each. A save is 392 KB at turn 20;
parse and stringify take 3 ms, so the cost is localStorage room (about 250 turns).
*Fix:* drop `elsewhere`; `regroup()` handles either form.

### Not a problem

- **Engine and AI** — the profile is flat (`fieldOf` 9.6%, GC 8%, `kindsUnder`/`unitNear` about 6.5% each).
- **Campaign turn** — about 99% is the off-table battles, already optimised; `aftermath` under 1 ms,
  `developRival` about 1 ms.
- **Per-frame allocations** — negligible; GC 0.2–0.8%.
