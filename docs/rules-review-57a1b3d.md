# Rules review at 57a1b3d

A fresh clause-by-clause review of the game against the rulebook (`PMC_2670.md`, 5,501 lines), at `57a1b3d` (11 October 2026). It follows the review at `a119ac2` (1 October 2026, since removed; it is in the history at `cb8e2a7^:docs/rules-review-a119ac2.md`). Book references are rulebook line numbers (`l.`); code references are at `57a1b3d`. Each finding was checked in the code, and most were run. Findings marked *ambiguous* are ones where the book can fairly be read the code's way; both readings are given, and they are the owner's call. Nothing was changed while reviewing.

What the owner decided or kept after the last review is not reported again (section 4), and every item fixed then was checked again (section 5). Finding numbers carry on from the last review, so no key is reused.

Severity: **H** changes outcomes often or breaks a scenario; **M** wrong in a common case, or a missing rule or player choice; **L** an edge case, a small number, or a tooltip, hint or card that misstates the rule.

## 2. Medium findings

- **REB-6 [M] Online campaign: a Rebel player's tactic is chosen twice, so Human Wave's free units can be taken twice, or kept under another tactic** — Book: l.2961 "the Rebel player may choose one of the following tactics for the battle"; l.2973 "may field 2 additional infantry units per Priority Level". Code: `camp-cmds.js:283-286` (`contractPick`) checks the roster pick under the contract's tactic, so 2 Battle-Tier infantry per PL come free; then `camp-world.js:169,190` start the battle with `tactics: {A: null…}, askTactics: true`, throwing that tactic away, and `engine.js:550-551` asks again at the table. `legalTactics` (`engine.js:513-520`) judges the list with no tactic, so an over-budget list is still offered every tactic. Effect: at BT III PL1 a 24-point pick, legal under Wave on the contract, takes Wave again at the table and calls up 2 more units (10 units, 30 points against 18), or takes Last Stand or Guerillas and keeps the 2 free units. Single-player and one-screen hotseat campaigns are not affected. (verified: ran `review/REB/wave_online.js`)
- **XEN-16 [M] Dual-mode Weapons works only within the shooter's own 12" sight, so it almost never helps a Gamma squad** — Book: l.4620 "When shooting at targets in LoS, units with Indirect Fire special rule get all standard modifiers"; l.4148 (Mental Projection) an enemy seen by one unbroken Xenotripod "is also visible to all other units in the Xenotripod army". Code: `src/rules/xeno.js:40` `dualMode` requires `hasLoS(state, a, t)`, which fails beyond `sightRange` (12", `space.js:515`), and `shoot.js:137` then forces Basic Firepower. Effect: a Core Gamma (range 30", minimum 6") firing on a target 20" away down a clear line, seen by a Beta 5" from it, shoots Basic (total 7); moved to 10" the same shot gets every modifier (total 10). Dual-mode only works from 6" to 12", and half range (15") can never apply. (verified: ran `review/XEN/dual.js` → at 20" basic, total 7; at 10" full, total 10) [ambiguous: "in LoS" may mean the unit's own 12" Limited Senses sight (the code's reading), or any target visible to the tribe with a clear line, which is the more natural reading for a 30" weapon.]
- **SOL-9 [M] Reasonably Defensive does not seek cover: a unit in the open with a target just stands and fires** — Book: l.4779-4781 "Unit seeks cover, but will engage the enemy if possible. That said, the soldiers are more concerned with their safety than the effectiveness of their fire." Code: `src/engine/ai.js:1079-1081`: a Defensive unit with a shot scoring > 0.4 fires from where it stands; moving to cover (l.1084, `scoreSpot` l.1092ff) is tried only with no such shot. Effect: an OpFor squad in the open, enemy 12" away and woods 2–6" behind, fires from the open rather than Advancing into the woods and shooting; Neutral (`neutralHold`) does take that cover, so Defensive is less cautious than Neutral. Defensive comes up on 1–2 (1–3 in Decapitation). (verified: ran `review/SOL/behave.js` → 9/10 stayed in the open and fired)
- **SOL-10 [M] Decapitation: OpFor leaders are often placed in the open, not in defensive positions** — Book: l.4983 "The OpFor Command Units are placed in defensive positions (e.g. in buildings or trenches) no closer than 12" to each other and to the table edges." Code: `src/rules/solitaire.js:682-698` looks only at building/bunker/ruins/woods and uses `spotNear`, but buildings and bunkers are impassable, so a leader never garrisons one; barricades and trenches (including the scenario's own D6 barricades) are never considered; failing all that, a random passable spot (l.692-697). Effect: on most tables the immobile, unbreakable leaders stand in open ground, which makes the scenario much easier. (verified: ran `review/SOL/decap.js`, 25 games on each of 8 planets → 176 of 288 leaders without cover; dense 25/39, desert 36/38; `decap2.js` → never inside a building)

## 3. Low findings


### The battle (l.1–725)

- **BAT-4 [L] The Move hint says Movement +2" for units that actually move Movement +4"** — Book: l.566 "The unit may move up to its Movement parameter + 2""; Riders (p.94), Human Wave (p.95), the Runners honour and doctrine T3 raise it to +4". Code: `src/engine/actions.js:153-155` builds both Move hints from `u.move + 2`, while the move itself uses `u.move + moveBonus(u,'move')` (`actions.js:473`, `engine.js:807`). Effect: a Rider gang (M10) is told "Move up to Movement +2" — 12"" and reaches 14"; likewise Human Wave infantry, Runners and T3 support teams. (verified: ran `review/BAT/hints.js`)
- **BAT-5 [L] The Reserve phase brings on scenario reserves and Battlefield Insertion units as two separate alternations** — Book: l.549 "When both players have reserve units, apply the Alternate activation rule, but ignore Overwhelming numbers." Code: `src/engine/arrivals.js:21-23` runs all of `scenarioArrivals` (alternating, l.206-262) before `afterArrivals` (insertion and held-back units, alternating, l.72-90). Effect: if B has the initiative and only insertion units and A has scenario reserves, all of A's come on first, then B's; arrival order decides who keeps 12" from whom and who draws the free shot (l.551). (verified: read)

### Vehicles, aircraft and drones (l.726–973)

- **VEH-4 [L] Troops in a vehicle slung under a Lifter survive when the Lifter is shot down** — Book: l.926 "When an aircraft suffers more Damage points than its Structure parameter, it is destroyed immediately… All troops on-board are killed or severely wounded, and are counted as destroyed". Code: `damage.js:205-214` (aircraft branch of `applyDamage`) kills only `t.cargo` (the slung vehicle), never that vehicle's own cargo. Effect: a Rebel Lifter carrying a Light transport with a squad aboard is shot down; the squad stays `alive`, aboard the dead truck, never returns, still counts as alive for victory (`scenarios.js:216`), and with no `lostAboard` mark comes home untouched in a campaign. (verified: ran `review/VEH/lifter.js`)
- **VEH-5 [L] A Tier III–V hull drives through a high wall for free, but pays 2" for a low wall** — Book: l.764-765 "vehicles from Tiers III to V can destroy any destructible linear terrain by moving into it (these moves are still subject to penalties for moving through terrain)"; l.1012 "2" in case of vehicles … when crossing a section of linear terrain". Code: `rules.js:862` gives `wall` `movePenalty: 0`; `move.js:77` lifts `impassable` for Tier III+ hulls, so it costs nothing. Effect: a Light combat vehicle goes 10" through a high wall but 8" through a low wall. (verified: ran `review/VEH/walls.js`) [ambiguous: a high wall's table penalty is "impassable", not a number.]
- **VEH-6 [L] Bailing out of a knocked-out vehicle is placed by the computer, not the player** — Book: l.789/792 "All troops on board immediately disembark"; Disembark l.827 "The unloaded troops are placed up to 4" from the vehicle". Code: `damage.js:263-293` (`dropOff`) picks the spot itself (best cover, then furthest from the enemy); normal Disembark lets the player tap it. Effect: the player cannot choose where the crew of an abandoned or burning APC lands, which decides their cover against the follow-up fire. Same gap as the fixed XEN-7 (teleport). (verified: read)
- **VEH-7 [L] Martyrdom hits on a vehicle use the shooting damage table, not the assault table** — Book: l.849-857 assault table for hits on vehicles (2-3 = 1 DP, 4-6 = D3); l.3541 Martyrdom "in the first round of each Assault … automatic D3 hits (resolved as normal)". Code: `assault.js:120-123` uses `resolveDamage` (shooting table) for machines. Effect: 1.0 DP per hit on average instead of 1.33. (verified: read) [ambiguous: "resolved as normal" may mean the shooting table.]
- **VEH-8 [L] The Fire! hint promises +1 and +2 to a shooter that fires Basic, and halves the wrong range for a dug-in gun** — Book: l.906 "Aircraft always shoot using their Basic Firepower"; l.633 at Basic Firepower "the D10 roll for shooting is modified ONLY by Firepower and the number of models" (mortars, artillery, aircraft, Always Basic Firepower units). Code: `src/engine/actions.js:160-161` always shows "Stand and shoot: +1 to the firing roll, +2 inside {range/2}"" without checking `shotMods(...).basic`, halves `u.range` rather than `shotRange(u)`, and prints it unrounded where `shoot.js:200` rounds up. Effect: a Flexible Strike Craft is promised "+1 … +2 inside 9"", a Remote mortar team "+1 … +2 inside 24"", and neither applies; a dug-in gun is told half of 48" instead of half of 24". The odds card itself is right. (verified: read; ran `review/BAT/hints.js` for the mortar) (Found by both the VEH and BAT reviewers.)

### Terrain, generators and scenarios (l.974–1484)

- **SCN-4 [L] Invasion is given to the defender as soon as no attacker unit is on the table and the defender holds all three zones, even with the second wave still in reserve** — Book: l.1419 "If the defender controls all landing zones, the second wave cannot arrive on that turn … but the attacker is allowed to roll again in subsequent turns, until the battle ends or the reinforcements finally arrive"; l.1279 units in reserve "are treated as being perfectly fine"; l.1413 the game ends only on the end roll after turn 12. Code: `scenarios.js:713-717` (new since a119ac2) returns `winner: def` in any End phase when no attacker unit is on the table and every zone is the defender's; the comment calls it "a reading" and the hint (l.660) says so. Effect: the first wave is wiped out in turn 2 with three defender squads in the zones; the battle ends at once as a defender win although half the attacker's force is still in reserve and not annihilated (l.1271). By the book the defender has to keep every zone hot in each Reserve phase from turn 4 to the end roll. (verified: ran `review/TER/scn_fail.js` → turn 2, winner B, "the landing has failed") [ambiguous: with nothing on the table the zones only cool if the defender walks out, so the outcome rarely changes; ending early is a shortcut the book does not give.]
- **SCN-5 [L] The move preview tells a vehicle (or Riders) that area terrain costs 1"** — Book: l.1013 "a unit deducts 1" (2" in case of vehicles) … moving into or through a piece of area terrain". Code: `src/view/input.js:168` (`movePreviewCard`) prints the TERRAIN table's `movePenalty` (1"), while `terrainCost` charges 2" for vehicles and Riders. Effect: a tank's preview of ending in a wood says "1" off the move" while the reach shown was worked out at 2". (verified: read)
- **SCN-6 [L] The board tooltip states the 4" capture rule over Invasion landing zones, where the house rule counts only units over the circle** — Book: l.1281 (capture within 4"), as changed by the SCN-1 house rule. Code: `src/view/input.js:268` shows "hold it with an unsuppressed unit and no enemy within 4"" for any point near an objective, landing zones included, while `holderOf` with `area.strict` (`scenarios.js:243-251`) ignores units outside the circle. Effect: the tooltip tells an Invasion player that an enemy within 4" of a zone denies it; one just outside the circle does not. (verified: read)

### General special rules and PMC units (l.1485–2528)

- **SPR-5 [L] A Destructive Weapon breach on a Markerlight-called shot gives the target its Stealth bonus back** — Book: l.1585 "Markerlights ignores the Stealth rule" (again l.1622); l.1527 a breach makes the target suffer "hits without any Defensive bonuses". Code: `shoot.js:350-354` recomputes Defence on a breach with `defenceAgainst(state, a, t, { noCover: true })`, dropping the `marked` flag that `shotMods` passed (`shoot.js:241-242`), so `rules.js:1352-1357` adds Stealth again (and loses `defensiveFire`). Effect: a marked Forward Observer team in a building, Defence 10 before the roll (8 + cover 2, Stealth ignored); an MCV answering the Mark rolls a natural 9, blows the building down, and the Defence becomes 11 (8 + Stealth 3). Blowing the cover away makes the shot worse. (verified: ran `review/SPR/breachmark.js` → 10 before, 11 after, for mark and designate)
- **SPR-6 [L] A breach on a vehicle behind a wall destroys the wall but gives no +1 on the damage rolls** — Book: l.1527 "the terrain piece is destroyed … Additionally, in such cases add a +1 modifier when resolving hits against the unit." Code: `shoot.js:363-367` (machine branch) calls `resolveDamage` without the `breach` +1, which only the infantry branch adds (`shoot.js:376`), though `shelterOf` treats a hull behind a wall as sheltered and the wall comes down. Effect: an MCV rolls a 9 at an LCV behind a low wall; the wall falls, but a damage roll of 5 stays a plain hit instead of a critical. (verified: ran `review/SPR/breachveh.js`) [ambiguous: a vehicle takes no cover from a wall, so the clause may be meant for troops only — but then the wall should not fall either.]
- **PMC-4 [L] A Rapid insertion platform cannot stop the enemy taking an objective** — Book: l.2425 "Cannot capture objectives and does not count towards victory conditions"; l.1281 capture needs "no enemy units within 4" from it (Broken units and flying units do not count)". Code: `scenarios.js:245` `holderOf` drops units with `holdsGround` false before counting deniers; the tooltip 'No Objectives' says "Cannot take or contest objectives". Effect: a platform 2" from an objective does not stop an enemy squad holding it. (verified: read) [ambiguous: "does not count towards victory conditions" can fairly cover denial too.]
- **SPR-7 [L] The Sappers and Destructive Weapon tooltips say "any wall or building"** — Book: l.1593 and l.1525 list "low walls, high walls (sections up to 6" long) and destructible buildings". Code: `ruletext.js` 'Sappers' "carry charges against any wall or building"; 'Destructive Weapon' "Can Demolish walls and buildings"; the engine rightly makes reinforced walls and bunkers immune (`destruct.js:22-26`). Effect: the tooltip suggests a reinforced bunker can be breached. (verified: read)

### Campaign and appendices (l.2529–2870, 5119–5501)

- **CMP-7 [L] Unreliable is never rolled for an AI-controlled unit, and a player's unit skips it on the search and sabotage actions** — Book: l.2805 "Before any action, the unit has to roll a D6: on a 1, the action is wasted and the unit does nothing." Code: the only check is `src/engine/actions.js:456-469` in `chooseAction`; `aiAct` (`ai.js:848ff`) never goes through it, and `checkarea` (l.441-453) and `sabotage` (l.455) return before the roll. Effect: in a solo campaign a rival unit with the Unreliable trauma never wastes an action; a player's Unreliable unit searching or sabotaging is never stopped. (verified: ran `review/CMP/unreliable.js` → with every d6 a 1, `aiAct` still advanced 4.5"; the same unit as a player's Move is stopped)
- **CMP-8 [L] Tactical Dumbness only removes cover; the unit keeps the hill and high-building Firepower bonuses** — Book: l.2803 "The unit does not get any bonuses from terrain." Code: only `coverFor` checks the flag (`rules.js:1261`); `shotMods` adds "firing from a hill +2" (`shoot.js:213-216`) and the high/reinforced building +2 (l.218-220) regardless. Effect: a Dumb squad on a hill shoots at 9 where the book gives 7; the card (`campaign.js:147`) promises "no bonuses from terrain". (verified: ran `review/CMP/dumb.js`)
- **CMP-9 [L] Rapid Relocation rounds "up to ½" up, against the TER-7 ruling that "up to" shares round down** — Book: l.2725 "the player is allowed to relocate up to ½ of their units". Code: `engine.js:820-825` `relocCap` = `Math.ceil(n/2)`, while Tactical Flexibility (`swapAllowance`), Mimicry and Underground Advance round down (TER-7, BUG-2, XEN-9). Effect: with 5 units, 3 may relocate; by the owner's reading, 2. (verified: read) [ambiguous: p.27 rounds every division up, the code's reading.]
- **CMP-10 [L] A depleted roster caps the Battle Tier for both sides instead of having to be rebuilt** — Book: l.2582 "The maximum Battle Tier is equal to the Company Tier of the weakest PMC"; l.2654 "If a player is unable to field a legal army due to losses, they must recruit new units or promote existing ones". Code: `camp-contract.js:20-30` (`fieldableTier`, `maxBattleTier`) lowers the cap to what the roster can field now; `rebuildNeeds` (`camp-aftermath.js:552-558`) only warns (`dossier-hub.js:242-246`) and `beginContract` does not block. Effect: a Tier III company that lost its Tier II–III units keeps taking contracts, each capped at Tier I, holding a full Tier III opponent (e.g. hotseat Player 2) to Tier I battles until it chooses to rebuild. (verified: ran `review/CMP/rebuild.js` → 200 rolls all Tier I) [ambiguous: the cap avoids a deadlock when the player has no money.]
- **CMP-11 [L] The Tough Negotiators card tells a PR Masters company it gets the lower pay on a draw** — Book: l.2715 "PR Masters. The Company counts all draws as victories"; l.2596 the winner takes the higher roll. Code: `src/view/dossier-after.js:285-293` (`rollsLine`) prints "In a draw both are paid the lower roll" and the lower total; `payment()` (`camp-contract.js:189-193`) rightly pays PR Masters the higher. Effect: a company with S2 and S5 sees the wrong pay when deciding whether to re-roll. (verified: read)
- **CMP-12 [L] The Surrounded, but Steady card leaves out the Rally-phase limit** — Book: l.2781 "When making a rally attempt in the Rally phase". Code: `campaign.js:123-124` "When rallying, the unit rolls one extra die…"; the rule (`rules.js:1839-1847`, `inRally`) rightly skips a Pass/Regroup rally since the CMP-2 fix. Effect: the card suggests it helps a Regroup. (verified: read)
- **CMP-13 [L] Suicidal Tendencies switches off Field Medics for that unit** — Book: l.2801 "ignores hits on 1-2 ('Steady, boys!'), but suffers 'Man down!' on a 5-6"; l.1545 Field Medics turn a 6 into 'MEDIC!' (1 SP) with a D6 save. Code: `damage.js:54-61` checks `suicidal` before `medics`, so a Suicidal unit near medics gets no MEDIC! and no save. (verified: read) [ambiguous: the book never says which replacement table wins.]

### Rebels (l.2871–3600)

- **REB-11 [L] "Death or Glory, Comrades!" tooltip leaves out an exclusion** — Book: l.2914 "Broken units, other units with this rule and units two or more Tiers higher … cannot benefit". ### Xenotripods (l.4103–4751)

- **XEN-17 [L] Infamy of Panic fires a second time when an already-Broken friend runs off the table** — Book: l.4674 "When a friendly unit within 18" is Broken or destroyed, the unit receives D6 Suppression points"; l.716 a broken unit that leaves the table "is counted as having fled", not destroyed. Code: `src/rules/xeno.js:128-137` `panicSweep` fires for any dead unit without `panicDead`; `runOff` (`endphase.js:40`) sets `alive=false`, and the break set only `panicBroke` (`xeno.js:112`). Effect: a friend breaks (D6 SP), then flees off the table in the next Rally phase (another D6 for the same break). (verified: ran `review/XEN/panicflee.js` → 5 SP on the break, then 3 more when it fled)
- **XEN-18 [L] Markerlights mark through blocking terrain by Mental Projection; shooting may not** — Book: l.4148 Mental Projection makes a seen enemy "visible to all other units"; only Indirect Fire may "shoot through LoS-blocking terrain". - **XEN-19 [L] Complex Teleport Network's free set ignores the one-Teleport-set and three-machines caps** — Book: l.4604 "one free Teleport Turret of Tier per Priority Level"; l.4520 "No more than 1 Teleport Turrets set per army"; l.4122 "no more than 3 turrets (or turret sets) and/or aircraft per Priority Level". (verified: ran `review/XEN/network.js` → ok) [ambiguous: "free" may mean outside every limit.]
- **XEN-20 [L] A Xenotripod aircraft brought down triggers no Psychic Bond** — Book: l.4158 "When a Xenotripod unit losses a model, each friendly unit within 6" gets a single Suppression point"; strike aviation is "normally piloted by one Xenotripod" (l.4385). ## 4. Owner decisions

These were set by the owner at the last review and are not reported as findings:

- Priority Level is capped at 2. One-player solitaire is PL 1, and co-op is PL 2.
- Each unit is a single token: no choice of casualty and no coherency.
- Secure and Takeover objectives are points, held from 4" away, with no terrain bonuses.
- The computer places the Secure objectives and the Demolish objective.
- Troops inside a transport take no landing Suppression, since transported units cannot be Suppressed (p. 36).
- "Command Units" means the units in the book's *Command units* group, not only those with the Command Unit (X) rule: Field command 4th grade counts towards the one-per-Priority-Level cap, and a Coordinate chain cannot activate it. The same goes for the Rebels' First Among Equals units (REB-3), every Alpha squad and every Leader Bug unit.

Also kept as readings or house rules after the last review, and checked to still match the code: TER-3, TER-5 (house rule), TER-8, TER-9, TER-10, SCN-1 (house rule: a landing zone is held only by a token over its circle), SCN-3, SPR-2, PMC-1, PMC-2, CMP-5, REB-1, REB-3, BUG-7, BUG-8, BUG-9, XEN-2, XEN-15, SOL-7, APX-1; and VEH-2 and VEH-3 as decided.
